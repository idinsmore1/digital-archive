## Review
A really solid album by Twenty One Pilots, and probably my favorite of theirs. The first half of this album slaps hard out of the gate, especially with the opener "Jumpsuit" and the following "Levitate." I also really like "My Blood" and "Chlorine". What brings this album down is the middle stretch, with only "The Hype" being a standout track for me personally. While the emotional factor is definitely there with songs like "Smithereens" and "Neon Gravestones", I just am not in love with the melodies on those in particular. However, the album ends incredibly strong in my opinion, with my favorite track "Bandito", and then two great songs back to back in "Legend" and "Leave the City." Overall, I think this is a really strong album by ToP, and the album of theirs that I come back to the most.
