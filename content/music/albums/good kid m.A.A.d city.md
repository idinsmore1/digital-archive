## Review
One of my favorite rap records of all time. You can see every detail of Kendrick's journey through Compton in this album, with his lyrical mastery apparent through the entire tracklist. The imagery is fantastic, the beats hit hard and fit so well, and most songs manage to be either insanely memorable or incredibly introspective in the best way. I've been listening to this album since high school, and it still feels just as great today up against any modern release.
