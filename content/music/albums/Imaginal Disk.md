## Review
An out of nowhere AOTY candidate for me. I had never heard of this band before this, but gat damn this album goes so hard. It' so insanely groovy, catchy, and different from any other pop album I've heard in a long time. The electronic and progressive influences are all over this album, and it's done so well that it just blends in and sounds amazing. Every time I put this album on, I basically listen to it front to back. Probably one of my favorite pop albums of all time.
