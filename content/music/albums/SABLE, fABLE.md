## Review
Easily my favorite Bon Iver album since [[For Emma, Forever Ago]]. Justin has not sounded so emotional, clear and inspired in a very long time. The SABLE EP that starts off this album is fantastic, and then essentially every single new song that was added in the fABLE section is amazing. This album is the spring to For Emma's Winter, the coming out of a darkness and feeling grateful and happy to be alive. Honestly I would put this album up there with [[music/artists/Porter Robinson|Porter Robinson's]] [[music/albums/Nurture|Nurture]] as one of the most evocative, "spring" sounding albums I've ever listened to. I have not loved a Bon Iver project this much in quite some time, and I hope that this style continues in Justin's future work, as I loved this entire record.
