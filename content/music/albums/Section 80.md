## Review
Kendrick Lamar's first studio album, and my introduction to his music! Yes, I'm going to put on my hipster hat here and say that I listened to Kendrick before [[good kid m.A.A.d city]], and I'm proud of it (shout out Anthony Fantano for his review of this album that made me check it out). 

This was the first album that really got me into rap as a genre, as I didn't realize how thoughtful and expressive so much of rap was/is, because all I had been exposed to up until that point was what had been on the radio. This album changed all of that for me, and in turn introduced me to one of my favorite artists of all time. 

This album just has so many great beats, and Kendrick strikes a great balance between his signature thoughtful rap style and being accessible in a catchy way. The tracklist on this album is just so thoughtfully constructed, it really is an album that once you throw it on, you feel like you should listen to the whole thing from front to back. "Hol' Up" and "A.D.H.D." are two songs that immediately grab your attention at the start of the album, and set that tone.
There are some less standout tracks in "No Make-Up (Her Vice)" and "Tammy's Song (Her Evils)", which, while having some important topics, just aren't my favorite songs instrumentally. However, the back half of this album after "Chapter Six" is just great song after great song. "Ronald Reagan Era" was my favorite song on first listen, and it's still one of my favorites. "Keisha's Song (Her Pain)" is still one of the most hard hitting tracks Kendrick has ever made to me. "Rigamortus" shows off Kendrick's insane rapping skill, I still have no idea how he raps so long without a breath in this song. "HiiiPower" to end this album is just insane, this track is a perfect foreshadow of the force that Kendrick would become in the coming years. It has the hard hitting social commentary, great beat, and incredibly catchy instrumental. 

Yeah, I love this album, and it will always be special to me.
