## Review
My favorite rap album of all time. It took some time to "get" it, but damn, now that I do, it's hard to listen to another rap album (or album in general) and think that it can compete with TPAB. The jazz riffs, great beats, lyrical mastery, musical tapestry in general across this album is basically unmatched. The poem that runs through the album, concluding with an "interview" with Tupac, is just one of the most insane and creative things I've ever heard in my life. The fact that Kendrick was able to make something even better than [[good kid m.A.A.d city]] is astounding, and solidified him as my favorite rapper, and one of my top 5 artists of all time.
