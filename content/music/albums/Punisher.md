## Review
Honestly, this album means so much to me. I first listened to it during the COVID-19 pandemic in 2020, and it's otherworldly sound and unmistakable atmosphere transported me to a place that felt familiar but different at the same time. It was a refuge during a very scary and uncertain time. Oh yeah, and the songs are all killer on this, packed with Phoebe's emotional, trademark falsetto voice. This is one of the very few albums that I consistently come back to, in a wide array of emotional states. `Moon Song` is one of my favorite songs of all time, no question, and `I Know The End` and `Graceland Too` are not far behind. Probably my favorite Indie album of all time, and one of my favorites in any genre ever.
