## Review
The ultimate spring album/dopamine booster/feel good experience. I love this album with my whole heart, and it helped get me through some incredibly difficult times during the COVID-19 pandemic. On the first nice day of spring every year, I feel compelled once again to put this album on, and it never disappoints. Porter's emotion and struggle through a dark period in his life is clear in his songwriting here, but he found his way through and show's it with the soaring melodies on basically every song here on the album . "Wind Tempos" is just a bit too long and slightly unnecessary to me in the context of this album, and while "dullscythe" and "blossom" are still good, they are just not standouts to me. Everything else is honestly fantastic. I love this album so much, and it made me even more of a Porter fan than I already was.
