## Review
The album that made me a Beyonce believer. This album slaps. Front to back, the groove quite literally does not end, with fantastic transitions from track to track. I was not a big Beyonce fan before this album, but I gladly played this thing on repeat all the time when it came out and still do now. The pure energy and fun that comes through in this album is so addictive that you can't help but move and dance when listening. A real triumph and I'm excited for whatever Beyonce does next. (Update, [[music/albums/COWBOY CARTER|COWBOY CARTER]] was great.)
