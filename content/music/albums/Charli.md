## Review
An amazing pop album and my introduction to Charli xcx. The futuristic, glitchy, hyperpop sound on this record is super infectious and fun to listen to. The emotional points like `White Mercedes` and `Official` hit hard too. Definitely the album that made me realize I really like good pop music.
