## Review
An album that for me, while it is very **good** in all senses, does not live up to the **greatness** that most of the albums in Kendrick's discography live up to. Most of these songs blend into each other too much for my liking, to the point that on re-listen while writing this review, I could not remember which song was which for a lot of the album besides the standout tracks. None of them are bad (except "YAH." Kendrick's shouting of "YAH" on that track just really doesn't do anything for me), but most just don't pop out to me. Sure there's great tracks like "DNA.", "ELEMENT.", and "HUMBLE.", and I expect that out of an artist of Kendrick's caliber, but this album feels like too much focus was had on the introspective side instead of the musicality, which just isn't my favorite vibe. I just would so much rather listen to [[music/albums/Section 80|Section 80]], [[good kid m.A.A.d city]], [[To Pimp A Butterfly]] or [[GNX]], so it's hard for me to place this in the upper echelon of Kendrick's music career.
