## Review

An absolutely **massive** country-esque release from Beyonce, and the Act II of her trilogy, with Act I being [[RENAISSANCE]]. This tracklist is 27 songs long (including short interludes) and has a runtime of 1 hour and 18 minutes, which is absolutely crazy. 

What's even crazier, is that for the vast majority of this album, Beyonce crushes it. This album has so many great songs on it, and they all fit the vibe of the album. Her cover of [[music/artists/The Beatles| The Beatles']] classic Blackbird is great, showcasing her amazing voice, immediately followed by the powerhouse "16 CARRIAGES" which is just a great all around song, and a great single leading up to this album. "PROTECTOR" makes me want to cry in the best way every time I listen to it. "TEXAS HOLD 'EM" was a smash hit for a reason, it slaps, and so does her cover of Jolene, with a banger in "BODYGUARD" mixed in between! "II MOST WANTED" has one of the best melodies and chorus' of any song in 2024, with incredible harmonies between Miley and Beyonce. 

I don't feel like writing about every song on this massive tracklist, but here's the gist - it's really really good and you should listen to it. I can't wait for Act III of the trilogy.
