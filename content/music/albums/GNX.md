## Review
A surprise release, absolute banger album from my favorite rapper ever, Kendrick Lamar. Came out in less than 24 hours from releasing a teaser, and did not disappoint in any shape or form. In my opinion, this is the album that [[DAMN.|DAMN.]] wanted to be, but didn't live up to. It's the perfect blend of thoughtful, introspective Kendrick, and Kendrick just having fun rapping over crazy beats with amazing hooks and features. "luther", "reincarnated", and "tv off"are 3 of my favorite Kendrick songs ever, without question. As always, I can't wait for what Kendrick does next.
