## Review
An album that I will always think of as a classic of my childhood. This album has meant so much to me ever since I first heard it as a teenager, and I listen to it every winter on loop without fail. Justin Vernon's pained, emotional voice is mesmerizing on this album, and even though it's pain and heartbreak he's going through here, there's a stillness to it. A sense that life continues, even through the hardest parts, and there is always light in the deepest pits, as shown with "Re: Stacks" at the end of the record. Anytime I'm feeling wistful, saddened, or down, I can throw this on and feel like I'm not alone. One of my favorites.
