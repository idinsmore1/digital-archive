This is an updating list of all the albums that I've felt compelled to write about, either for good or bad. I've scored them, and each album in the table has a link to my review for the album,  a link to the artist so you can see how this album holds up to the rest of theirs that I've reviewed, as well as a link to Spotify to check the album out for yourself.  

For all you fellow data nerds - don't expect the scores in this list to be normally distributed, tend to write far more about the albums I love than the ones I don't 😀
## my rating system
My rating simple is pretty simple and attempts to be as repeatable as possible. For each song in the tracklist, I give the song a rating between 1 and 5 - attempting to match the scores given to the following criteria:

**1** - Absolutely hate the song. Will not listen to it, even in a straight listen through of the album.  
**2** - Actively dislike the song. Will occasionally listen to it when listening to the whole album.  
**3** - Neutral on the song. Don't love or hate it, usually don't skip.  
**4** - Actively enjoy the song. Will not skip when it comes on vast majority of the time.  
**5** - Absolutely love the song. Will not skip.

If I'm really stuck between two scores, I'll add **.5** to the lower rating. If there is a short intro, prelude track, or something of that nature, I usually give it a 5 unless it's actively unlikable. 

I sum all of the individual track scores, divide by the number of songs in the album, and multiply by 2 to get the score out of **10**. I find for me, that this is the most fair way I can compare albums without *too* much bias one way or another.
