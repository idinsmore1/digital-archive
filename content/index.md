> *[And] so do all who live to see such times. But that is not for them to decide. All we have to decide is what to do with the time that is given us.* - Gandalf, **The Fellowship of the Ring** - J.R.R. Tolkien

Hi! I'm **Ian Dinsmore**, a data analyst in my late twenties working in bioinformatics. I have way too many interests to keep track of in my head, so I write about them here instead. I'm not particularly good at talking about myself, but if you want to learn more, you can find everything about me [[about me|here]].

This site is built with [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) and [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/) - two fantastic free tools I'd recommend to anyone who loves diving deep into topics and sharing their thoughts. 

I made this site because I love learning, exploring, and talking about things I enjoy. As I get older, I continue to realize that if you love something, you should celebrate it. That's what I want this to be, a celebration of all the things that I love about life, personally and philosophically. People like to call these things wikis, digital gardens, and second brains, but I view it differently. I'm not looking for this site to be the answer to my own productivity crisis, or to feel like I'm better than everyone else because I'm writing things down (I'm certainly not, and I'm still **so** bad at taking notes). 

I want this to be a continuation of myself, a way for me to cherish the things I love, and talk about why there are things I don't. I want to be able to look back in 20 years and remember all the music I loved now, or the books I may have read, and I know my brain alone can't handle that. 

With that being said, here a few things that you can find on this site!
